Get SS1 Tool for Android

SS1 Tool for Android is on Google Play. Buy it once, and Google Play installs and updates it like any other app.

  1. Open SS1 Tool for Android on Google Play on your phone or tablet, and buy it.
  2. Open SS1 Tool. Read and accept the License Agreement.
  3. On Connect, type your SuperStation's IP address (shown at the bottom of the MiSTer menu, or in Console Mode's network settings) or tap Find my SuperStation. The default login is root / 1.

Updating

Google Play updates SS1 Tool by itself. To check now, open SS1 Tool's page in the Play Store, or in SS1 Tool tap About › Check for updates.

New phone or tablet

Sign in with the same Google account and install SS1 Tool from the Play Store (Manage apps › Library). You don't pay again. Saved devices aren't copied: add your SuperStation again on Connect.

Problems?

  • "Your device isn't compatible": SS1 Tool needs Android 8.0 or newer.
  • Something doesn't fit the screen or doesn't work: in the app, open About › Report a problem with the app. It saves a report file and opens a GitHub issue for you.
  • Refunds and orders: see the refund policy, or email f3.digital.systems@gmail.com.
  • Anything else: SS1 Tools support.
