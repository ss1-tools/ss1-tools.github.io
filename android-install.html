Install SS1 Tool for Android

SS1 Tool for Android comes from this website, not the Play Store, so Android asks you to allow the install once.

  1. Buy the app on the Android page. You'll get an email with your license key and a download link.
  2. On your phone or tablet, open the download link and download SS1Tool-Android-x.y.z.apk.
  3. Open the downloaded file (from the download notification, or Files › Downloads).
  4. If Android says your browser or file manager isn't allowed to install apps, tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source, then go back.
  5. Tap Install. If Play Protect shows a warning about an app it doesn't know yet, tap More details and Install anyway. SS1 Tool for Android has no ads, trackers or access to your personal files.
  6. Open SS1 Tool. Read and accept the License Agreement, then enter your license key. That's it: the key is checked once, and the app works offline from then on.
Check the download (optional). The email lists the file's SHA-256 checksum. On a PC, certutil -hashfile SS1Tool-Android-x.y.z.apk SHA256 shows it, so you can make sure the file is exactly the one we published.

Updating

Download the new version from the link in your purchase email (Lemon Squeezy also lets you get it again from your order) and install it over the old one, the same way. Your settings, saved devices and license stay as they are.

Moving to a new phone

Your key works on up to 3 devices. To free up a device you no longer use, open About in SS1 Tool on that device and choose Remove license from this device, or contact support.

Problems installing?

  • "App not installed": an older copy signed differently may be on the phone. Uninstall it first, then install again.
  • "There was a problem parsing the package": the download didn't finish; download it again.
  • Android older than 8.0: the app can't be installed; see supported versions.
  • Anything else: SS1 Tools support.
