SS1 Tool for Windows

Free. One exe, no installer. It connects to your SuperStation One over your home network and opens in its own window.

What it does

SetupSS1 scripts, Update All on the stable lane, Samba, scraper and RetroAchievements accounts, and the MiSTer settings editor with backups.
VideoEvery video setting explained, a wizard for how your TV, monitor or CRT is connected, a symptom fixer, and safe try-and-keep.
SD cardFlash the official SS1 SD Card Installer, check partitions and exFAT health, deep read/write tests, and full SD backups.
NetworkEthernet details, WiFi setup, and playing games from a network share, including one shared from your PC.
DevicesUSB devices, Bluetooth pairing and backups, controller mappings, and a live controller tester.
SupportOne debug report for the Taki Udon Discord, with passwords, WiFi names and addresses removed.

The full feature list and the source are on GitHub.

Requirements

  • Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit
  • A SuperStation One on the same network as your PC
  • Flashing an SD card needs administrator rights (SS1 Tool offers to restart itself as administrator)

Before you download

SS1 Tool changes settings and files on your SuperStation and can erase and flash SD cards. Please confirm:
