Refund Policy

For SS1 Tool for Android · Effective October 9, 2026

14 days, no questions asked

If SS1 Tool for Android isn't right for you, you can get a full refund within 14 days of buying it. You don't need to give a reason.

How to ask for a refund

  1. Open your purchase receipt email from Lemon Squeezy, or your order on Lemon Squeezy's My Orders page.
  2. Use the contact or support link there and ask for a refund, with your order number.

Refunds go back to the payment method you used. Lemon Squeezy processes them; how long the money takes to arrive depends on your bank or card.

After a refund

A refund ends your license: your license key is deactivated and the app stops working on your devices. Please uninstall it.

After 14 days

After 14 days, purchases are final, except where the law where you live gives you a right to a refund. If the app doesn't work on your device, please report it first: we'd like to fix it.

Chargebacks

Please contact us before disputing a charge with your bank; we'll sort it out. A chargeback deactivates the license key.

SS1 Tool for Windows

SS1 Tool for Windows is free, so there's nothing to refund.
