Set up, fix and support your SuperStation One

SS1 Tools are unofficial community tools for SuperStation One owners. They connect to your SuperStation over your home network to set it up the right way, sort out picture and controller problems, and collect what's needed when you ask for help.

SS1 Tool for Windows Free

The full toolkit, from your PC.

  • First-time setup wizard, from flashing the SD card to WiFi and Update All
  • Video settings explained, with safe try-and-keep for HDMI, CRT and more
  • SD card and NVMe checks, backups and the official SD card installer
  • Controller tester, remote control, file manager and debug reports
SS1 Tool for Windows

SS1 Tool for Android $4.99

The same tools in your hand, made for touch.

  • Connect, Setup, Video, Network, Files, Devices, Remote and Diag
  • On-screen controller and keyboard for the SuperStation
  • Fits any phone or tablet, with large text and accessibility settings
  • On Google Play: one-time purchase, no ads, no tracking, no subscription

Why SS1 Tools?

Stable updatesKeeps Update All on the stable lane, so a Linux update can't force you to reflash your SD card.
Safe video changesTry any video setting: unless you press Keep, the SuperStation puts the old one back by itself, even when the picture is gone.
Faster helpOne debug report with versions, settings and storage health, with passwords, WiFi names and addresses removed, ready to post in the Taki Udon Discord.
Your data stays yoursNo ads, no analytics, no tracking, and we will never sell your data. Privacy policy
